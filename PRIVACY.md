# Shidur privacy policy

*Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the Shidur app for Android (`io.github.eyalshimshi.shidur`). [עברית](#מדיניות-פרטיות)*

Shidur doesn't collect, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

## What stays on your phone

- Your favorite stations, recently played stations, stations you added by address, settings and wake-up alarm.
- Downloaded station logos and the station list.
- If the app crashes: a description of the crash (app version, Android version, phone model and the error).

This information stays on your device and isn't backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Who the app connects to

To work, Shidur connects to these services, which see your device's IP address like any website you visit:

| Service | Why |
|---|---|
| The radio stations' streaming servers | To play the station you choose, and, when the app opens, to get your likely stations ready (Instant start; Data saver turns it off) |
| GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) | To download updates to the station list |
| The addresses of station logos, mostly Wikimedia (`upload.wikimedia.org`) | To show station logos, once per logo. You can turn logos off in Settings |
| Google Play services | Only when you cast to a Chromecast or similar device |

Shidur sends these services nothing about you beyond what any app needs to download a file or a stream.

## Crash reports and problem reports

Nothing is sent automatically. After a crash, Shidur offers to copy the crash description or to open a pre-filled report on GitHub in your browser. You can read and edit it, and it's only sent if you choose to submit it. The same goes for "Report a problem" and "Suggest a station". Reports on GitHub are public, and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | Playing streams; choosing lower quality on mobile data when Data saver is on |
| Foreground service (media playback), wake lock | Playing in the background with the screen off |
| Notifications | The playback controls notification |
| Alarms & reminders, run at startup | Ringing the wake-up alarm on time, also after a restart |

## Children

Shidur isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, and its history is public on GitHub. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/EyalShimshi/shidur/issues>, or write to the email address on Shidur's Google Play page.
